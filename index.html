About Us

LPU Lost & Found Portal is an official student initiative built to help students and staff recover lost belongings inside the LPU campus. Our mission is to connect finders directly with owners — fast, secure and without any third party.

Every day items like ID cards, wallets, earphones, lab coats and keys are lost in blocks, hostels and cafeterias. This platform connects the finder with the owner without any third party involvement.

Our Mission

To create a trusted, verified platform where every lost item can find its way back to its owner quickly.

How It Works

Report lost or found item, add photo and location, and directly connect via contact details after verification.

Campus Only

This portal is exclusively for Lovely Professional University students, faculty and staff.

Secure & Verified

Secret key verification and admin moderation ensures only genuine owners can claim items.
